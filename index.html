Expert Guidance

Avoid These Pitfalls

Practical tips to help you succeed. Pick a topic below.

Avoid these common mistakes that can derail your project. Here's how to stay on track:

Procrastination & Poor Planning

Break tasks into daily chunks with a timeline; use tools like Google Calendar.

Ignoring Instructions

Highlight key requirements first and check against them at every draft.

Weak Research & Plagiarism

Use APA tools like Zotero; paraphrase and aim for 5+ credible sources per page.

Poor Structure & Grammar

Outline first (thesis → evidence → summary); proofread with Grammarly or a peer.

Overlooking Formatting

Stick to APA 7th edition, double-space, 1-inch margins, Times New Roman 12pt.

Supervisors reject ~30% of undergraduate projects for avoidable reasons. Here's how to prevent it:

Unaligned Topic

Choose a feasible, relevant topic early and get pre-approval from your supervisor.

Shallow Literature Review

Review 20+ recent sources; highlight your unique angle and contribution.

Methodological Flaws

Detail methods precisely, e.g., "Survey of 100 students using stratified sampling."

Incomplete Submission

Use a checklist: abstract, chapters 1–5, references, plagiarism report (<15% similarity).

Late or Sloppy Presentation

Submit early for feedback; format consistently throughout your document.

A confident defense comes from thorough preparation. Follow these key strategies:

Know Your Work Inside Out

Read your entire project multiple times. Be able to explain every section without notes.

Anticipate Questions

List 10 likely questions and practice your answers with a peer or mentor.

Prepare a Clear Presentation

Use slides that summarize key points — objectives, methodology, findings, and recommendations.

Dress and Present Professionally

First impressions matter. Dress appropriately and speak clearly and confidently.

Stay Calm Under Pressure

It's okay not to know everything. Acknowledge gaps honestly and offer thoughtful reasoning.
